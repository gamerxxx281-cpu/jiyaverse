# JiyaVerse ✨ | Special Cheer-Up Edition

A deeply personalized, interactive cheer-up web experience handcrafted for **Jiya Bhola** ([@officialjiyaverse](https://www.instagram.com/officialjiyaverse/) & [@jiyabholaofficial](https://www.youtube.com/@jiyabholaofficial)).

Designed to be casually handed to her like a reel or surprise digital gift that brings an instant smile to her face!

---

## 🌟 What's Inside the Experience

1. **🎬 Instant Reel / Story Mode (`Watch Reel Mode`)**:
   - Tap the top button to launch a full-screen, vertical Instagram-story / Reel viewer.
   - 7 curated animated story cards featuring her warmest smiles, puppy cuddles, competition victories, and cheering affirmations with tap-to-advance navigation and auto-progress bars.

2. **🎵 Ambient Web Audio Lo-Fi Player**:
   - Zero external audio files required! Synthesizes soft, dreamy Lo-Fi Rhodes chords (Fmaj7 - Em7 - Dm7 - Cmaj7) with warm analog filter simulation.
   - Interactive sound effects (crystal chimes, harp notes, pop bubbles).

3. **💊 Emergency Serotonin Dispenser**:
   - An interactive widget that drops randomized personalized pick-me-ups, warm puppy facts, virtual hot cocoa, and loving reminders whenever she taps it.

4. **🌈 Rainbow of Truths (Affirmation Arcs)**:
   - Direct interactive recreation of her rainbow coloring page:
     - *I AM STRONG • I AM CAPABLE • I AM UNIQUE • I AM KIND • I AM APPRECIATED • I AM HEARD • I AM IMPORTANT • I AM LOVED*
   - Tapping each arc plays an ascending musical chime and reveals an empowering message.

5. **📸 The Polaroid Scrapbook (21 Curated Photos)**:
   - Aesthetic tilted polaroids with washi tape details and handwritten captions.
   - Categories: *🐾 Puppy Love, 🌸 Floral Magic, ✨ Chic & Cozy, 🏆 Victories, 🤍 Special*.
   - Fullscreen lightbox with zoom and an interactive **❤️ Send Love** reaction counter that bursts floating hearts.

6. **🎨 Jiya's Cozy Coloring Corner (Interactive Digital Coloring Studio)**:
   - Features 12 of her actual favorite coloring drawings from the uploaded collection (Snoopy in hammock, Autumn Snoopy with tea, SpongeBob & Patrick, Bedroom morning glam, "I Am Manifesting Good", "Bit of a Mess, Doing My Best", French sidewalk cafe, Stitch cuddles, Rainbow affirmations, Knight hug, Movie & Popcorn night).
   - Tools: **🪄 Magic Tap Fill** (flood-fill between lines), **🖌️ Freehand Brush**, **🧽 Eraser**, **Size Slider**, **16 Pastel Swatches**, **↩️ Undo**, and **💾 Save Artwork** button!

7. **💌 Wax-Sealed Heartfelt Letter**:
   - A 3D interactive envelope with a heart wax seal that opens and smoothly slides out a touching personal letter reminding her she never has to carry the world alone.

8. **🦦 Floating Mascot Otter**:
   - Bounces in the corner and drops funny, sweet motivational one-liners when tapped.

9. **🌙 Starlight / Rose Theme Toggle**:
   - Switch between warm rose-gold glow and cozy starlight night mode.

## 🚀 How to Run & Preview

### Option 1: Direct File Open
Simply double-click `index.html` in your file explorer to open it immediately in Chrome, Edge, or Safari!

### Option 2: Local HTTP Server (Recommended for full canvas download features)
Open PowerShell or Terminal inside this folder:
```powershell
python -m http.server 8000
```
Then visit: `http://localhost:8000`
